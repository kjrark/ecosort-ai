    STEM Methodology

    Scientific conversion factors powering every EcoSort AI metric.

    1 kg PET plastic recycled = 1.5 kg CO₂e saved + $0.35 recovered

    Mechanical recycling of PET avoids virgin resin production. Virgin PET emits ~2.9 kg CO₂e/kg vs. ~1.4 kg for recycled flake — a net saving of ~1.5 kg CO₂e, plus commodity resale value.

    1 kg organic/food waste diverted = 2.5 kg CO₂e saved

    Landfilled food waste anaerobically decomposes into methane (CH₄), a greenhouse gas 28× more potent than CO₂ over 100 years. Composting or rescue avoids those landfill methane emissions entirely.

    1 kg aluminum recycled = 9.0 kg CO₂e saved

    Recycling aluminum uses only ~5% of the energy of smelting new metal from bauxite ore — the single largest carbon lever in household waste.

    1 kg cardboard recycled = 0.9 kg CO₂e saved

    Recycled fibers cut pulp demand and landfill volume. Note: grease-soaked cardboard contaminates paper streams and must be composted instead.

    1 Li-ion battery diverted = 50 EcoPoints (hazard premium)

    Lithium cells can cause landfill fires and leach heavy metals. Certified e-waste hubs recover lithium, cobalt, and nickel for reuse — highest-impact action in the system.

    The Master Formula

    Total CO₂e Saved = Σ (kg diverted × material factor)

    Value Recovered = Σ (kg recycled × commodity $/kg)
